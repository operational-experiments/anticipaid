method

Similarity is cheap. Equivalence is not.

01

Ingest

Fetch public open-market metadata from each venue and normalize binary Yes/No probabilities.

02

Constrain

Reject obviously incompatible candidates, including conflicting numeric terms, and require substantial token overlap.

03

Score

Combine token-set overlap with sequence similarity. Only high-scoring candidates survive into the public comparison.

04

Refuse certainty

Even a strong lexical match remains a candidate until settlement semantics are explicitly reconciled.

why this exists

The interesting signal is often between markets.

A single prediction market gives you one aggregate forecast. Several markets asking nearly the same question can expose information latency, participant differences, liquidity effects, fee structure, or—most importantly—hidden differences in what the contracts actually mean. Anticipaid is built to make those disagreements legible.
